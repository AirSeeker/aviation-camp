academic risk management perspective. For example, Figure B-1 depicts the PAVE checklist. Many of the tools described in

training and guidance on the appropriate use of these tools.

pilot or airplane related, including such factors as weather,
air traffic control (ATC), navigational aids (NAVAIDS), terrain,
takeoff and landing areas, and surrounding obstacles. Weather
is one element that can change drastically over time and
distance.

Additional Risk Assessment Tools
The CARE checklist provides a breakdown of rationale pilots use during the 3P “process” stage [Figure B-6]. It includes a
perspective of what could happen (consequences), what may be done to prevent an unwanted outcome (alternatives), a check
of the actual conditions (reality), and an analysis of pilot motivation (external pressures).
