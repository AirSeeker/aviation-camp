Appendix C Density Altitude Calculation
To determine density altitude:
1. Set the aircraft’s altimeter to 29.92 inHg. The altimeter will indicate pressure altitude.
2. Read the OAT.
3. Mark the intersection of pressure altitude (diagonal) and temperature (vertical) lines on Figure C-1.
4. Read the density altitude from the horizontal lines on Figure C-1.
