<strong>SERA.2001 Subject </strong>
Without prejudice to SERA.1001, this Annex applies to airspace users and aircraft: 
(a) 
operating into, within or out of the Union; 
(b) 
bearing the nationality and registration marks of a Member State of the Union, and operating 
in any airspace to the extent that they do not conflict with the rules published by the State 
having jurisdiction over the territory overflown. 
<strong>SERA.2005 Compliance with the rules of the air </strong>
The operation of an aircraft either in flight, on the movement area of an aerodrome or at an operating 
site shall be in compliance with the general rules, the applicable local provisions and, in addition, when 
in flight, either with: 
(a) 
the visual flight rules; or 
(b) 
the instrument flight rules. 
<strong>GM1 SERA.2005 Compliance with the rules of the air </strong>
<strong>APPLICABLE LOCAL PROVISIONS </strong>
Applicable local provisions include local aerodrome regulations that are published in the relevant 
Aeronautical Information Publications (AIPs). 
Such local aerodrome regulations may contain requirements for the operation of the aircraft 
transponder on the movement area of an aerodrome with the intent to ensure provision of 
surveillance data to the air traffic services unit providing services at the aerodrome, as well as other 
aerodrome units (e.g. apron management services). 
<strong>GM1 SERA.2005(b) Compliance with the rules of the air </strong>
<strong>GENERAL  </strong>
When determining whether to operate in accordance with the visual flight rules or the instrument 
flight rules, a pilot may elect to fly in accordance with instrument flight rules in visual meteorological 
conditions, or may be required to do so by the competent authority. 
<strong>SERA.2010 Responsibilities </strong>
(a) 
Responsibility of the pilot-in-command 
The pilot-in-command of an aircraft shall, whether manipulating the controls or not, be 
responsible for the operation of the aircraft in accordance with this Regulation, except that the 
pilot-in-command may depart from these rules in circumstances that render such departure 
absolutely necessary in the interests of safety.

(b) 
Pre-flight action 
Before beginning a flight, the pilot-in-command of an aircraft shall become familiar with all 
available information appropriate to the intended operation. Pre-flight action for flights away 
from the vicinity of an aerodrome, and for all IFR flights, shall include a careful study of available 
current weather reports and forecasts, taking into consideration fuel/energy requirements and 
an alternative course of action if the flight cannot be completed as planned. 
<strong>SERA.2015 Authority of pilot-in-command of an aircraft </strong>
The pilot-in-command of an aircraft shall have final authority as to the disposition of the aircraft while 
in command. 
<strong>SERA.2020 Problematic use of psychoactive substances </strong>
No person whose function is critical to the safety of aviation (safety-sensitive personnel) shall 
undertake that function while under the influence of any psychoactive substance, by reason of which 
human performance is impaired. No such person shall engage in any kind of problematic use of 
substances.
