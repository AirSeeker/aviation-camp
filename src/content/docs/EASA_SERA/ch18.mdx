# Chapter 18

of the Air (SERA) Figure AP2-1 3. OPERATING LIMITATIONS AND EQUIPMENT REQUIREMENTS 3.1.

A heavy unmanned free balloon shall not be operated without authorisation from the ANSP(s) at or through any level below 18 000 m (60 000 ft) pressure-altitude at which: (a) there are clouds or obscuring phenomena of more than four oktas coverage; or (b) the horizontal visibility is less than 8 km.
